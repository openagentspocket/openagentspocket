NOT through `writeEncoded`: the gate, probe filter and diagnostic need the bytes in JS, and the SSH stream's undecoded `onEncodedData` is optional (absent on fakes). Typing and the terminal's own replies (device attributes) go to the PTY. Not verified on a device (integrator).
