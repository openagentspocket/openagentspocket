Agents Pocket shows the coding agents running on your own machines and lets you answer them from your phone. Open source, no accounts, no tracking.

What it does

Every agent at a glance

Working, blocked or done: every agent on every Host, with its repository, branch and changes.

Answer when they wait

Approve a command or pick an option with one tap, read the chat, or open the terminal.

Notifications only you can read

Your Host encrypts each notification with a key only your devices hold. The relay cannot read it.

Straight to your machines

The app talks to your Hosts over SSH. Your code, terminals and files never pass through a server of ours.

How it works

  1. Run your agents in herdr on your Mac or Linux machine.
  2. Install the Agents Pocket plugin and show a pairing code.
  3. Scan it with the app. Your Host shows up with its agents.
