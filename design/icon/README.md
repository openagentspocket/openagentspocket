# OpenAgentsPocket icon, final (C · Cards)

Generated by `gen_final.py` (`python3 -I gen_final.py 26`, Chrome headless; 26 = fan angle). Folder: `design/icon/` (this one)

## Colors
- Background solid `#000000`
- Pocket `#6E6E6E`; front card `#FFFFFF`; side cards `#FFFFFF` at 55% and 80% opacity
- Gaps between cards, and between cards and pocket, are real cut-outs (masks), not painted strokes, so the mark works on any background and in monochrome

## Small-size refinement
Fan of 3 narrow cards (+/-26 degrees, 270 px wide) rising well above a shorter pocket, with 26 px gaps; the front card plus the two side edges still read as three at 29 px and as a "face" (centre card + ears) at 60 px.

## Files
- `ios-icon-1024.png`: iOS icon, 1024x1024, opaque, unmasked (iOS applies the corner mask). `hasAlpha: no`.
- `android-foreground-1024.png`: adaptive foreground, transparent, mark scaled to fit inside the 66% safe circle (diameter 676 px, 3% margin).
- `android-background-1024.png`: adaptive background, opaque `#000000`.
- `android-monochrome-1024.png`: Android 13 themed icon, single black silhouette on transparent, same safe zone, gaps kept.
- `splash-1284x2778.png`: full-screen splash, mark centered on solid `#000000` (mark about 50% of the width). Opaque.
- `splash-icon-1024.png`: transparent mark only (about 90% of the canvas), for Expo's splash `image` with `backgroundColor: "#000000"` and `resizeMode: "contain"`.
- `favicon-48.png`: 48 px, rounded corners, transparent corners.
- Previews and sources: `contact.png` (180/60/29 px on light and dark), `preview-{180,60,29}.png` (masked), `icon-flat.svg`, `android-foreground.svg`, `gen_final.py`.

Caveat: the 1024 foreground is white cards on transparent, so it looks blank in viewers with a white background; check it over a black background.
